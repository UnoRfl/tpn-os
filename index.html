Our story

Tyo Paeng Nyo started in 2003 with one small kitchen, one family recipe book, and a stubborn belief: that home-cooked Filipino food deserves a proper home. Twenty years later, we're still cooking from the same recipes, still using the same suppliers, still answering the phone ourselves.

The name comes from our founder — Tyo Paeng. The "Nyo" is for you. It's how everyone in Las Piñas has always said it: "Punta tayo kay Tyo Paeng, nyo." Come, let's go to Tyo Paeng's.

We cook every plate like it's for our own family. We treat every customer like a regular, even on their first visit. We charge fair prices for honest food. And we don't take shortcuts on the things that matter — the lechon kawali is still hand-fried, the sinigang is still simmered for hours, the pancit is still made fresh per order.
